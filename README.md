# Brutal Seas — demo
Commercial fishing tycoon demo (Bering Sea king crab + Pacific tuna grounds). leafactivity.com · Leafactivity@gmail.com

Price ranges (approximate ex-vessel $/lb, used as daily-seeded ranges):
- Red king crab $14–24: NPFMC BBRKC SAFE 2025 Appendix F (2024/25 avg $22.60/lb); Alaska Fish News Dec 2025 (record king crab prices).
- Snow crab $3.2–6: approx. recent Bering Sea opilio ex-vessel; Alaska Fish News 2025 (snow crab +11% YTD).
- Golden king, tuna, mahi, halibut, cod, shrimp: rough industry estimates for gameplay, not quotes.

Deep-sea creature prices ($5k–$250k each) are fictional game values.
Queued next: depth-tied upgrades (boat: longer winch cable, bigger crane, more cages, chilled tanks for full rare-creature price; sub: depth rating, cages per dive, lights, rare-signal sonar, battery) and rarest creatures gated to deepest zones + matching upgrades.

Boat specs are approximate real-world figures (length, hold, pots/gear, fuel, crew, speed) for gameplay; in-game "sets" represent strings/sets of real gear.
Audio: all sounds are synthesized live with WebAudio (no third-party audio files, no licensing needed).
